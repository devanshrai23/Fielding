# Sources of data
## device_info.zip
The device_info is gathered by the "FedScale: Benchmarking Model and System Performance of Federated Learning at Scale" authors and available at https://github.com/SymbioticLab/FedScale/tree/master/benchmark/dataset/data/device_info.

## fMoW_data.zip and country_or_zone_samples_in_2015-2018.json
The fMoW data is based on the original Functional Map of the World (fMoW) dataset gathered by the "Functional Map of the World" authors and available at https://github.com/fMoW/dataset.
